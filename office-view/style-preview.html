Office Style Preview

The same scripted run in two art styles: Nova scouts an idea, you approve it, work is handed down the line, Vex finds a bug, the team huddles and learns a rule. In the real view, this script is replaced by the event log.

View
Speed

A. 16-bit pixel art

Cozy, retro, top-down. Characters are tiny sprites.

  • Fastest to build and extend: a new agent is one small sprite.
  • Charming and readable at small sizes; a good fit for many tiny characters.
  • Less "product-like"; furniture detail is limited by the tiny canvas.

B. Isometric, flat color

Clean, modern, 3D-ish angle with soft shadows.

  • Looks polished and dashboard-friendly; depth makes the office feel like a place.
  • Scales to detailed rooms and richer furniture.
  • More work per character and prop; animations take longer to tune.
